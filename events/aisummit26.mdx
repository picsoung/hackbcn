faq:
  en:
    - q: How does the AI Summit hackathon work?
      a: >-
        It is a two-day, in-person build at Norrsken House Barcelona. Saturday covers registration, the opening keynote, team formation, workshops, and hacking. Sunday finishes with submissions, project demos, judging, and awards.
    - q: Who can participate?
      a: >-
        Developers, designers, entrepreneurs, and AI enthusiasts of all skill levels are welcome. No-code, vibe-code, and full-code builders can all take part.
    - q: Do I need coding or AI experience?
      a: >-
        No. HackBarna is about learning and building together. Workshops and mentors will help participants get started, whether they use prompts, AI tools, or a full-stack codebase.
    - q: What can I build?
      a: >-
        Build an original AI-related app, website, game, tool, or prototype that follows the event theme and guidelines.
    - q: Can I work on an existing project?
      a: >-
        To keep the playing field level, teams should start a new project during the hackathon. You can brainstorm and prepare ideas before the event.
    - q: What if I do not have a team?
      a: >-
        No problem. Team formation and networking are part of Saturday morning, so you can meet other participants and build a team at the event.
    - q: How big should my team be?
      a: >-
        Teams should have no more than three people. If you arrive with a larger group, split into multiple teams or use the event to meet new teammates.
    - q: When and where does the hackathon take place?
      a: >-
        The hackathon runs September 19 and 20, 2026, at Norrsken House Barcelona.
    - q: How do I register?
      a: >-
        Use the Register button on this page to continue to the official AI Summit Barcelona hackathon registration.
    - q: What should I bring?
      a: >-
        Bring your laptop, charger, and any technical accessories you need. Food and drinks will be provided during the event.
    - q: Can I participate remotely?
      a: >-
        No. AI Summit 26 is an in-person event at Norrsken House Barcelona.
    - q: Who owns the intellectual property for my project?
      a: >-
        Participants retain ownership of their intellectual property. Organizers may request repository access to verify that the project was built during the event.
    - q: Is there a Code of Conduct?
      a: >-
        Yes. The HackBarna Code of Conduct applies to everyone. Be respectful; harassment and abuse are never tolerated.
