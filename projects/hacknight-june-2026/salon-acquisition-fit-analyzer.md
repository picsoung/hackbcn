A tool that scores independent hair salons in Barcelona as acquisition targets, using Google Places data on reviews, digital presence, and location, combined with manual owner and succession details. It outputs a one page report with a clear Pursue, Monitor, or Pass recommendation, backed by risk and opportunity breakdowns. Built to address a real, overlooked problem: aging salon owners with no succession plan, and no systematic way to evaluate which ones are worth approaching first.
