MatchMind is a gamified football trivia experience built with React and TypeScript. Players can jump into multiple quiz modes including Classic Quiz, Timed Match, Daily Challenge, Tournament, Survival, Guess Player, Guess Club, Timeline Challenge, and Top 5/7/10 list challenges.
The game lets users filter by subject, era, and difficulty, then compete solo, pass-and-play, or in team duel formats. It includes mode-aware scoring, streak and speed bonuses, XP, ranks, podium results, survival lives, countdown timers, and post-match answer review. The question bank spans players, clubs, nations, competitions, and tactics from the 1990s through modern football.
