Bufa is a daily Catalan kite forecast. Every evening it scans 8 spots from L'Estartit to the Ebro Delta, ranks them across the next 5 days, and pings a Telegram message when the wind's worth chasing —
with a map and 5-day dashboard at bufa.netlify.app.
