RenovAI pulls every scattered source of your renovation — WhatsApp messages, emails, photos, supplier quotes — into one structured database. It's natively compatible with Magicplan, so your floor plans and measurements flow straight in. Then your own AI assistant connects over MCP and answers anything about your project: what a contractor quoted, which room is behind, what's left to decide.
