FastAndSlow is an autonomous wildfire incident-response sandbox
 built around two complementary agents. Jev reacts quickly, scoring
 time-local hotspot evidence, opening simulated incidents, and
 assigning verification drones. Devin reasons more slowly about
 complex response plans. Every proposed route or action passes
 through a deterministic policy engine that can approve it, reject
 unsafe paths, or request a revision. The interface visualizes
 historical Deepfire satellite data, observed heat, nearby
 infrastructure, drone routes, uncertainty, and a complete audit
 trail.

 Operators can also join a real-time Vonage Video API briefing with
 Ari, a simulated field liaison. They ask questions by voice; SLNG
 transcribes and synthesizes speech, while a conversational model
 receives a fresh read-only snapshot of the replay and recent
 conversation history. This lets Ari explain why a drone was
 rerouted, what assets may be at risk, and where evidence is
 insufficient without repeating canned answers.
