Cherubin is a platform for hiring AI employeed to handle document heavy business tasks such as procurement, financial analysis, policy compliance and operations review. The user/manager can create digital employees assign multiple tasks in parallel to each employee and instrucut the digital employee to do tasks and then delete the digital employee once work is done. Many employees can be created and tasks for each can run in parallel, thereby increasing speed, accuracy and efficiency.
