The problem:
Short videos deliver opinions, predictions, and persuasion in a confident tone. Viewers have no quick way to tell what can be fact-checked and what is just framing.

What I built:
OpenFact takes a video link and returns an audit in simple, jargon-free language.

It shows:
1. The video's main intent.
2. Which claims are fact, opinion, prediction (guess about future), or moral judgment.
3. Persuasion techniques, if any used.
4. Which moral values the video appeals to.

It works as a web app and as a Telegram bot.
