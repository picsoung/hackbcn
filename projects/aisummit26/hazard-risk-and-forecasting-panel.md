Natural hazard data -wildfires, storms, floods, drought - sits in four separate official feeds, none of which tell you whether the place you're standing in, or heading to, is actually at risk. This is one map that answers that: it finds your location, shows a single combined risk picture from low to extreme, lets you search or click anywhere for the reasoning behind it, look a week ahead, ask about travel, chat about it in plain language, and get alerted when something serious appears near you. Behind it, the official sources are pulled continuously and scored onto a worldwide grid of cells, each carrying a risk level plus the facts that produced it and where a source can't reach a given day, the map says so rather than guessing.
