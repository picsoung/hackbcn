1. The Problem
Buying a home is the biggest financial decision of your life, but the process is overwhelming and opaque. Buyers struggle to calculate true monthly costs, local tax impacts (like ITP), and realistic affordability leading to anxiety, guesswork, or costly mistakes.

2. What We Built
H² (Hack House): A zero-friction real estate analyzer that turns any property URL into an instant, expert-level financial breakdown and clear buy verdict.

3. How It Works
a) Paste & Profile: The user pastes a property URL (Idealista, Fotocasa...) and enters basic financial inputs (income, savings, age, and region) for custom tax calculations.

b) Instant Engine: H² scrapes property data, applies regional tax cuts (ITP), runs mortgage stress tests, and analyzes neighborhood metrics.

c) Verdict & Action: In seconds, the user gets a clear verdict, a live financial simulator, an AI-powered voice/text chat for custom context, and WhatsApp/PDF sharing via Vonage.

d) CRM Dashboard & Interactive Map View: All analyzed properties are automatically stored in a centralized CRM dashboard. Users can toggle between a high-density comparison table and a split-screen interactive map with color-coded verdict pins to track, filter, and manage all market opportunities in one place.
