Finding something to watch often means endless scrolling through catalogs, even when you already have a rough idea of what you want.

PolTV turns movie and series discovery into a conversation. Tell Pol what you're in the mood for, refine it naturally—“more action,” “make it funnier,” “with cars”—and PolTV recommends real movies and series that evolve with your preferences.

But PolTV goes beyond a traditional recommender: it brings your viewing mood to life through generative video, with Pol starring in cinematic worlds inspired by the conversation. Viewers can also save recommendations to their profile and discover what others are watching through Around PolTV.

The result is a TV-first experience where you don't browse a catalog—you talk, explore, and watch your next movie night take shape.
