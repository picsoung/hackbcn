So we built ControlledFire: a live operations map running on real Deepfire data. It answers three questions — what's burning right now, where the next one might start, and what we should do about it, that last one generated by AI through Nebius. And the rule we followed through the whole codebase: if Deepfire doesn't give us a number, we don't show one.
