The Problem

Choosing what to watch together causes decision paralysis, endless catalog scrolling, and debate, while traditional search engines fail to understand colloquial or mixed-language requests.

What We Built

CouchSync is an AI living room TV voice sommelier that acts as an engaging host, listening to everyone in the room to deliver fast, consensus-driven movie and series recommendations.

How It Works

Natural Voice Input: Extracts moods, genres, and preferences from casual speech and colloquial slang in English, Spanish, or Spanglish.

Consensus Engine: Balances group preferences against the TMDB catalog to recommend top matching titles.

Host Dialogue & Safety: Delivers real-time recommendations with concise, TTS-optimized voice commentary protected against prompt injections and data leaks.
