The problem: AI now does our coding, our reasoning, our thinking. The skills we stop using are the ones we lose.

What we built with Thnky: a daily brain workout for the AI era. Coding, logic and critical thinking, in five minutes a day. A little thinking every day.

How it works: AI generates a challenge at your level. Stuck? Hints escalate (nudge, concept, approach) but never give the answer. Thnky trains you; it never solves it for you.
