The problem is that older people are usually not tech savy which makes them impatient, which makes them not use many features, including recommendations and better usage of tech in their disposal. The idea is to focus on what channels they are used to use and leverage those to make their life easier, mostly phone calls and voice.

The solution consists of a data pipeline to enrich and generate synthetic data for users and their recommendations on the one hand, while using slng to interact with voide locally for low latency, and phone calls are using vonage, the entire solution is GDPR compliant as it is being hosted in Europe by mastra and nebius as the backend model
