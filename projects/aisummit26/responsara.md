We use fire locations and data on their spreading to build a plan to minimize damage and human loss.

We identify infrastructure and people clusters within the radius of the fire spread across time, use LLMs hosted on Nebius Token Factory to assess their value and risk and then calculate priorities and firefighting crews' plan.

We believe nobody should be left behind. Not those who forgot to opt in. Neither those who aren't tech savy enough. However, we understand resources are limited. We use voice agents to create a synchronous communication channel to understand the level of assistance needed, escalate to human dispatchers when unclear or complex, suggest evacuation shelters to the public, then present the fire analyst with a clear plan for each crew based on people group size, asset value, proximity, road closures.
