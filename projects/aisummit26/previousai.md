Previous AI takes a home address and returns what threatens it: wildfire, flood, heat and avalanche, each scored 0–100 from public scientific and official data — ERA5, CMIP6, Spain's official SNCZI flood maps, IGN, Copernicus, the cadastre — with every figure carrying its source, period and resolution. It shows what already happened nearby, what it means for that specific dwelling, how the risk shifts by 2050, and an action plan: what to do, what to buy (real products and prices), and which public grants pay for part of it.

One rule holds it together: only data scores. AI narrates, illustrates and ranks advice — it never produces a number.

It also runs backwards: all 7,597 Spanish municipalities ranked, and every building crossed with the official flood maps, for the people who have to reach those homes.
