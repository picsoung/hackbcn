**Someone who has studied a language for months and still froze when it mattered — the landlord picked up, the interviewer asked a question, and nothing came out.**

The status quo is a year of study that doesn't survive contact with a real conversation.

with ling0, you rehearse a specific situation out loud — calling
the landlord, asking a price, sitting a job interview — against a generated scene that shows you what's being talked about. You speak; it listens.

Afterwards you can refine vocabulary and grammar in dedicated training modes to improve your understanding of the language.
