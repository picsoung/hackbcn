People have more financial apps, products and information than ever, but still struggle to know what actually needs attention. We built MyFinGap, a conversational financial diagnostic in Telegram that uncovers blind spots such as forgotten pensions, unknown fees, missing protection or unrealistic retirement goals.

What makes it different is the assessment itself: it is designed to capture both what people know and what they don’t know, turning uncertainty into useful financial insight. And the value grows over time, because each assessment becomes a baseline for the next one, showing what changed, what improved and which blind spots still remain.
