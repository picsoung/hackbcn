The problem: Streaming platforms give us thousands of movies, but deciding what to watch still takes too long. People end up endlessly scrolling, reading synopses, and opening trailers without actually making a decision.

What we built: ReelPick is a decision-making engine for movies. Instead of another catalogue, it shows you short video moments from movies that match what you want, so you can quickly understand the vibe and decide.

How it works: You describe what you’re in the mood for, and ReelPick gives you a small set of relevant options. As you like, skip, reject, or mark movies as watched, it learns from your feedback and adapts the next recommendations.
