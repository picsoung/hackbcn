Picking a film on a TV is a poster grid that ignores your mood, and watching it is passive. Reverie fixes both: Discover is a conversation over 27,839 real TMDB films that returns a shortlist and explains it; Movie Jam is a room where a host and guests join by QR and write a short film together — a generated or imported screenplay split into timed beats, where rewriting one beat re-derives the rest in a single model call. React + TypeScript on a TV-first layout, Supabase with row-level security enforcing every room rule in the database, Nebius for grounded reasoning that can't invent a film, fal.ai for generated video behind a server-side allowlist and a hard spend ceiling, Vonage for opt-in camera with a consent register.
