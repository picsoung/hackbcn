Online recipes are built for reading, not cooking, one mistake and you're on your own. Taula fixes that by teaching you a language and its culture through food. Pick a country (Catalonia, for our demo) and cook a real dish step by step, with voice guidance in the local language. Take a photo at each stage and AI checks your progress and helps you fix mistakes, never judging or roasting you. Levels move from ingredient words to full conversation, plus cultural facts and progress tracking
