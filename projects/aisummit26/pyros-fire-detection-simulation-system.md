PYROS turns raw satellite fire detections into instant, shareable operational reports. It pulls real-time thermal anomalies from NASA FIRMS (VIIRS), pinpoints and geocodes the fire, adds terrain, wind and fuel context, and runs a Rothermel spread simulation to estimate how it could grow — helping teams prioritise and act faster, seconds after detection. Waring to surrounding population centres and infraestructure in the way of the fire according to the algorithm will be implemented.
