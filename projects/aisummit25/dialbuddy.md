Problem
As a newly arrived expat, you regularly need to call government offices and clinics. Issues are voice-only, you don’t speak the language, and lines are often busy—so you end up redialing and wasting hours.

Solution
We call on your behalf in their language, auto-redial until connected, and confirm the booking using your details.
