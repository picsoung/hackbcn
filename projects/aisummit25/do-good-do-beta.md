An early-stage concept for "Elevate Sport" - a global scouting platform for women's sport (piloting with soccer/football). It uses AI to analyse player-uploaded videos and generate insights for both talent development and club recruitment.

Currently, women's clubs don't have access to sufficient data to enable effective recruitment. Instead, they rely on small geographies, or expensive and ineffective scouts.

Elevate Sport enables girls to upload videos which then triggers an AI generated analysis against set performance criteria. Clubs get access to a player database and can use this data analysis for player recruitment and informed decision making. The players can use the AI Coaching reports for playing development.

In the short term, this has the potential to instantly bolster talent recruitment for women's football clubs, and in the long term - it has the potential to completely elevate global women's sport.
