ARIA is an AI-powered real estate platform designed for developers and agencies to showcase their projects through immersive 3D experiences. It combines interactive visualizations, real-time availability, and a virtual sales agent powered by AI that understands buyer preferences, answers questions, and recommends the most suitable units - helping real estate teams convert leads faster and offer a personalized buying experience at scale.
