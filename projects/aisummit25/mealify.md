We are developing an intelligent app that, given a user's running profile sourced from third-party platforms such as Strava and their training goals, automatically generates a personalized training plan paired with a tailored nutrition plan.

The system is powered by an LLM-based agentic architecture and integrates the Glovo API to identify food products that meet the nutritional requirements of each plan. It accounts for both the caloric needs defined by the training program and the nutritional values of available products. Our agentic solution is equipped with tools to discover products on the Glovo platform and automatically add them to the user's basket for seamless checkout.

As a result, the app provides users with a comprehensive training plan and a ready-to-purchase Glovo basket containing all the necessary items to reach their goals. The user just needs to click "Buy"!
