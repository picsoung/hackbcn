NeuroPlan AI Campus is the first AI-powered educational platform designed for neurodivergent people, allowing them to pursue ESO, FP, or University according to their strengths, interests, and learning style. It combines adaptive education, vocational guidance, and regulated training in an ecosystem validated by Spanish educational regulations.

The system generates a NeuroAcademic profile through adaptive tests, automatically creates Individualized Educational Plans (PEI), and designs adapted curricula that comply with official credits and requirements. Content is delivered in visual, auditory, and interactive formats with alternative assessments and a specialized virtual AI tutor for neurodivergences like ADHD, ASD, and dyslexia.
