Every year, millions of business travelers face flight delays, cancellations, and booking errors... and most never receive the compensation or proactive support they're entitled to.

Claimwise was born to change that. It's an AI-driven travel protection layer that automatically detects disruptions, verifies eligibility for compensation, and handles claims directly through TravelPerk's legal partners, so travelers don't need to lift a finger.

But Claimwise doesn't stop at legal rights. It's built with two smart systems working together:

Lexi, the AI Legal Expert: detects when a flight is delayed or cancelled, checks regulation eligibility (like EU261), and triggers the claim process instantly.

AIDVS (AI Dynamic Verification System): proactively confirms hotel and flight bookings before departure, and alerts both the traveler and support team if any issue is detected, solving it before it becomes a problem.

The result? Fewer support tickets. Happier travelers. Higher retention for TravelPerk's business clients. And a new premium-tier feature that turns trust into revenue.
