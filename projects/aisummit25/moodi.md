Moodi is a mood tracking and reflection application built with SwiftUI and The Composable Architecture (TCA). It combines an iOS/macOS app with n8n Cloud workflows for AI-powered content generation.

Users record their mood through voice or text input, which gets transcribed and enhanced by AI. The system generates personalized images, audio, and video content based on the user's mood and input, with content safety filtering built in.

The app features reflection cards with 3D carousel effects, an interactive mood selector, and a recording interface that connects to multiple AI services including Runware for image generation, ElevenLabs for text-to-speech, and Veed for video synthesis.
