Yummit is an AI-powered adaptive nutrition coach that helps people stick to their meal plans by fitting them into real life. Unlike traditional nutrition apps that only tell you what to eat, Yummit adapts when and how you eat - syncing with your calendar, sending reminders only when you're free, and adjusting plans automatically when you skip or give feedback.

Built with Lovable, Linkup and Glovo, Yummit combines personalization, automation, and context awareness into one seamless experience. If you're too busy to cook, Yummit even connects you with nearby restaurants through Glovo - closing the gap between planning and action.

In short: Yummit turns nutrition from a rigid plan into a system that adapts to your day - so you can finally stay consistent.
