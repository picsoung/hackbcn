Evolv is an AI-powered growth coach designed to help people not just set goals - but achieve them. Most people struggle with personal development because motivation fades, life gets in the way, and goals often compete with each other. Evolv tackles these challenges by combining artificial intelligence, behavioral science, and agile principles to turn self-improvement into a measurable, accountable, and adaptive journey.

Through an intuitive mobile platform, users set their personal, professional, or financial goals, and Evolv breaks them down into small, achievable two-week sprints - just like a Scrum Master would for a product team. It then provides daily check-ins, adaptive feedback, and motivational nudges, keeping users engaged, consistent, and focused even when priorities shift or motivation dips.

What makes Evolv different is that it doesn't stop at tracking habits - it guides users, learns from their behavior, and adjusts their plans dynamically as their lives evolve. It also helps them see how their goals interact and overlap, offering a holistic approach to personal growth.

Our vision is to build the "operating system for human potential" - a platform that empowers individuals to make meaningful progress toward the life they want. With Evolv, personal growth becomes structured, supportive, and achievable - one sprint at a time.
