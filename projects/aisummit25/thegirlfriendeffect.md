Ever heard of the "girlfriend effect"? That magical style transformation when someone starts dressing better? We built an AI that detects if you've got it and upgrades you if you don't!

Upload your photo and our AI analyzes your style. If you don't have the girlfriend effect, it generates an upgraded version of you with better clothing and grooming. Then, AI extracts the clothing items and finds real products you can actually buy to achieve the look.
