KIKO helps you care for your dog or cat with smart, evidence-based advice and simple actions.

It keeps tab on your pet's health, suggests the right care, and even orders food when needed. Listens, understands your pet's data, and tells you exactly what to do next, with sources.

KIKO gives busy owners peace of mind: triage, reminders, and trusted advice in one app.
