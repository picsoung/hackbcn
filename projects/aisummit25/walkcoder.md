WalkCoder is your hands-free coding partner — a Telegram and Discord bot that lets you build, test, and iterate apps just by talking. While you walk or jog, WalkCoder listens, understands your voice, and gets the work done in the background. It connects through Hookdeck to manage messages and logs, speaks naturally with ElevenLabs, explains ideas using Groq and Linkup, generates media with HuggingFace and Veed, translates with Lingo.dev, and even writes, runs, and tests real code apps automatically. Your AI teammate that codes while you move. 🚀
