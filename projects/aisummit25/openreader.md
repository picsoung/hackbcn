It is a simple app where you can upload a file, book, website, blog, or any text, and the app will read it for you together with any way you prefer for learning and catching your attention such as:

- Text with Follow Along: a cursor pointing to the text that is being narrated, perfect for people that want to improve their reading habits.
- Text with Images: Same as before but adding images to represent the text that is being narrated.
- Text with Videos: Same as before but even more efficient for people that really have difficulties with focusing.
- Interactive Mode: Any of the modes before mentioned but in between it will ask you questions about the topic to make sure you are understanding.
- Summarize: For long text you can also summarize the content, or adapt it to your narration format.
- Share: once you have a narration fully rendered, you can share it with your friends and they can watch it for free!

The app will be open source: that is why is call OpenReader.
