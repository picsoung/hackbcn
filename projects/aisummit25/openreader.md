It is a simple app where you can upload a file, book, website, blog, or any text, and the app will read it for you together with any way you prefer for learning and catching your attention such as:

- Text with Follow Along: a cursor pointing to the text that is being narrated, perfect for people that want to improve their reading habits.
- Text with Images: Same as before but adding images to represent the text that is being narrated. Perfect for people that need a visual element to understand topic and to catch their attention.
- Text with Videos: Same as before but even more efficient for people that really have difficulties with focusing. The videos will catch your attention while the app reads you the content so you can finally learn and remember the content.
- Interactive Mode: Any of the modes before mentioned but in between it will ask you questions about the topic to make sure you are understanding.
- Summarize: For long text you can also summarize the content, or adapt it to your narration format, and then you can use any of previous modes.
- Share: once you have a narration fully rendered (VoiceOver/Images/etc). You can share it with your friends and they can watch it for free!

The app will be open source: that is why is call OpenReader. So people can add more modes or features. Also the app can run on desktop and use OpenSource TTS or AI generation so you can do it all offline, perfect for long flights or confidential information.
