Twice a day, every day, we ask ourselves: What do I eat today? Answering that question whilst keeping a varied diet and fighting guilty pleasures is really tough. Meet Green, the AI nutritionist that recommends the healthiest options in your city. Green knows your fitness goals and your order history in Glovo. By adapting to your recent workout and what you ate today, it recommends the best-suited meals for every moment of the week.
With the voice command and its integration with the Glovo app, you can fulfill an order in one click, just by talking to it.
