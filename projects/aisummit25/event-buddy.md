EventBuddy connects people through shared experiences. When your friend cancels last minute, post your spare ticket and choose from people who apply. Want to visit a museum or go hiking but have nobody to join? Find companions who share your interests. Our Spotify integration analyzes music compatibility between profiles, showing how much you have in common before you decide. Whether you’re sharing a concert ticket or looking for an activity buddy, we help you discover people with similar tastes and turn solo plans into meaningful connections and potential friendships.
