Imagine a tiny, brilliant robotic chef in your company's kitchen - blending data, ideas, and workflows like ingredients - and serving you fully custom internal features in just minutes, through iterations with the Ratatouille AI Agent (Dev Agent).

That chef is Ratatouille.ai - an artificial intelligence that crafts your admin system with the precision of an engineer and the flair of a master chef. Manage clients, suppliers, employees, products, or invoices in a backoffice so smooth it feels Michelin-starred.

But Ratatouille.ai doesn't stop there. Inside your software lives a native AI agent (User Agent), ready to help you add new ingredients whenever inspiration strikes: create new objects, query your database, automate backoffice tasks, or even redesign your workflow - just by asking.

Your system grows with you. It evolves. It adapts. And every new feature arrives like a fresh dish on the menu. Because at Ratatouille.ai, software isn't coded - it's cooked.
