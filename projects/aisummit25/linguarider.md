Language barriers hurt businesses in all verticals, specially in moments like last-mile deliveries, generating confusion and unaccounted costs for all parties involved, We created a live translation voice service for bridging gaps in communication, with async communication through voice notes and text. This helps reducing missunderstandings, in the most critical situations where language differences is the main barrier.
