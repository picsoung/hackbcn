Airlines owe €4 billion in compensation annually. Most people never claim it, the paperwork is designed to make you give up.

We flip it: AI predicts your delay before takeoff, pre-approves your claim, and auto-files while you're in the air. GPT-4 Vision reads your boarding pass, fills the forms, writes appeals when airlines reject you. Zero effort on user side end.

We're building this to solve problem we heard from travelers like ourselves and validate the pain point with TravelPerk team. But this solution can be implemented for all kinds of claims. We predict a problem, prep the solution, execute when it happens. Insurance claims after a car accident. Medical pre-authorizations before surgery. Warranty claims when your laptop dies. Anywhere there's paperwork between you and money you're owed, this model kills friction.

We started with flights because airlines trained us to accept losing. Turns out AI's better at their bureaucracy than they are.
