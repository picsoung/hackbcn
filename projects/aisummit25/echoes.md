Echoes is a fun and educational app that lets you bring back to life the great people immortalized in paintings and sculptures - and hear their stories told by themselves.

It's the perfect companion for your museum visits or city tours, enriching every experience with vivid, memorable narratives spoken in the first person by the protagonists of history.

Behind the scenes, Echoes combines cutting-edge AI tools: Google Vision identifies the artwork, Linkup crafts the first-person narration, Linguo ensures seamless translation into any language, Eleven Labs gives the story a natural voice, and Veed brings everything together into a captivating video.

All built with Lovable, Echoes transforms your encounters with art into living conversations.
