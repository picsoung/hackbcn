Two weeks ago, we were at AI Engineer Paris - running from one room to another, trying to catch great talks, take notes, and not miss anything.
By the end of the day, our brains were full… our notes were messy… and we realized: Conference experience is broken:

On one side, speakers are fighting for attention - staring at a sea of laptops and phones and a distracted audience.

On the other side, attendees like us are struggling to capture that idea you heard and liked. Your only real options are to:

1. Write down the exact sentence as the speaker just put it
2. Try to track down the person's online presence who talk about this idea / advice
3. Ask others if they took some notes

All of these “solutions” are time-consuming and unreliable

That’s why we built Little Birds — the Shazam of conferences.

We change the way people experiences and capture knowledge during events by bringing back attention to conferences.

Just open the app, place your phone on the table, and enjoy the talk.
Our little birds listen, capture, and transform what’s said on stage into personalized insights LIVE — tailored to your interests, your goals, and your job.

No typing. No distraction. Just vibing.

Little Birds is not only a note-taking app but a gateway to a world of knowledge, where each insight captured by a member becomes an opportunity to learn, share and connect with others
