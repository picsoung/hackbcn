Two weeks ago, we were at AI Engineer Paris - running from one room to another, trying to catch great talks, take notes, and not miss anything. By the end of the day, our brains were full, our notes were messy, and we realized: Conference experience is broken.

That's why we built Little Birds - the Shazam of conferences.

Just open the app, place your phone on the table, and enjoy the talk. Our little birds listen, capture, and transform what's said on stage into personalized insights LIVE - tailored to your interests, your goals, and your job.

No typing. No distraction. Just vibing.

Little Birds is not only a note-taking app but a gateway to a world of knowledge, where each insight captured by a member becomes an opportunity to learn, share and connect with others.
