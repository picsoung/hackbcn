Using voice or text input, users simply tell the AI what fruits they have and our system instantly generates three original ideas: a baked dessert, a drink, and a smoothie. Each comes with a recipe, zero-waste tips, and stunning, Gemini-generated visuals that look straight out of a digital cookbook.

But it's more than a recipe tool - it's a mindset shift. The platform encourages people to see beauty in imperfection, buy groceries on sale, and support local markets. Users can chat with a friendly AI cooking assistant, ask questions about substitutions, and learn how to reduce waste while still eating beautifully.
